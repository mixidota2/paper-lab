Core Idea

Author claim

Fictional author claim (there is no paper): replacing a learned attention mixer over history with a fixed additive exponential decay over session events preserves next-item ranking while cutting serving cost.

Research Bot interpretation

Our interpretation (not the authors’): if this were real, the interesting bit would be whether a one-parameter decay is a sufficient statistic for short-term intent, not the decay formula itself. This lab does not test that. The split on this page is the format: author claim stays on the left; our reading stays on the right.

Why It Might Work

Inference — not an author claim

Inference (labeled as such). A recency kernel might work when temporary intent is short compared with the session and when long-term preference is already captured by a slowly updated user tower. That is a guess, not evidence, and not an author quote.
