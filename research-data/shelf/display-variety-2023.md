# Display variety: observed experiment versus optimized counterfactual

## Source
Sachse, Oetzel, Klapper, *I'll Try That, Too*, CRC TRR190 Discussion Paper 404, 28 June 2023. DOI 10.5282/ubm/epub.103757. Source: https://rationality-and-competition.de/wp-content/uploads/discussion_paper/404.pdf . Institutional identity: https://epub.ub.uni-muenchen.de/103757/ . Manuscript dated 27 June; series cover dated 28 June.

## Bounded primary extraction
24 German stores, eight per group; 2016 weeks 2–12. Full 23-SKU display versus two 16-SKU variants; regular shelves retained full assortment. Discounts accompanied displays (pp.8–10). Random assignment is not stated.

Store-week log-sales DiD uses store effects and promotional controls (pp.13–16). Reduced-variety interaction: −0.137, SE 0.082, p<0.10; multiplier 0.872 versus full variety. This is brand unit-sales response, not storewide profit. Product selection and facings co-vary; isolated facing causality is not established.

Subsequent response-based optimization fixes 42 integer facings, 1–4 per SKU. Predicted profit improvement is 0.85%, using assumed costs, not observed retail margins (pp.20–22). No pre-intervention holdout or realized adoption of this optimum is documented in the inspected sections.

## Interpretation
Include only as qualified promotional-display field evidence plus subsequent counterfactual allocation. Not fixed-total cross-category proof; not a validated pre-intervention forecast-to-realized-profit chain.

## Independent numerical check
exp(-0.137)=0.8719702. Using the displayed SE, normal approximation exp(beta ± 1.96 SE)=[0.7425092,1.0240036]; includes unity. This interval is our approximation, not a reported paper CI and not a substitute for cluster-aware inference. Do not present marginal evidence as strong significance.
