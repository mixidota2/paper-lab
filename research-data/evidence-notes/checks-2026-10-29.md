# FeatureArc — primary review, October29 JST

Worth Reading for evaluation design, not a causal remedy or model ranking. Primary https://arxiv.org/html/2610.37434v1 ; metadata https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37434 . Submitted October27 15:45:50UTC; retrieved October28 18:13–18:15UTC. Entire main HTML sections I–VIII, Tables I–IV and figure captions read; figure pixels, raw trajectories, code and author experiments NOT TESTED. No linked official artifact located in the article or exact title/authors GitHub search. A bounded absence observation is not proof no artifact exists.

The important shift is from checking a latest extension to checking the complete accumulated feature on the agent's own final code. Of273 runs passing the last stage,69 failed whole-feature acceptance (25.3% rounded). Inheritance and regression have different conditioning sets. The paper evaluates73 tasks/263 stages/53repositories and10 configured agents; pooled first-stage62.7%, finalchain28.5%. These are reported results, not our reproduction.

Persistent workspace/session, sequential specifications, no hidden-gate feedback, no rollback. Gate success requires F2P AND P2P; final chain suites differ from the final-stage suites. A repaired chain may pass finally despite earlier failure. TableIV94.9% means missed prior failure among stages AFTER failure, not awareness/success. Inheritance72.9% is among failed later stages. Bootstrap intervals over tasks do not establish repeated-run stability. Configured model and harness are coupled; conditional comparisons are not randomized treatment effects. MajorityLLM trajectory labels were checked by a limited blinded first-author sample. No prospective outcome improvement from a testing intervention established.

Closest prior: SWE-STEPS, SlopCodeBench, EvoCode-Bench; existing TRACE tests verifier sensitivity, SWE-Serve tests serving E2E, FeatureArc adds inherited own-code feature dependence. ## Monitor Accuracy Isn't the Answer — evaluation limits
https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36544 ; https://arxiv.org/html/2610.36544v1
Submitted Oct27 02:59:21 UTC; announced Oct28. Full main §§1–8 and appendices A–G read. No raw artifacts, implementation, PDF visual audit, or execution.

For an independent clean restart with no overhead, G=(1−q)τp−qφ(1−p). Constant90% accuracy allows −5 to+35pp in a mathematical example, not a tested detector. The study contains194 executions on19 selected SymPy issues;130 traces support a retrospective audit. None of103 live comparisons is certified by its simultaneous binomial rule; this is not universal impossibility. Context reset yields0/20 successes versus1/20 and56.7% higher cost despite removing the protocol error label. Classical identities are attributed. Five draws/task, selected single repository, post-outcome audit design, no learned-monitor comparison and excluded verifier overhead limit inference. August31/September6 collection is not October production performance.

Own analysis: valuable bridge from progress-looking signals to action-conditioned value. Strongest transferable distinction is clean reset vs workspace-retaining reset vs continuation: each needs its own outcome law. Retrospective replay is not a prospective E2E policy improvement. Future work should hold recovery semantics and total overhead-adjusted budget fixed, test held-out tasks/models, and expose abstention rather than optimize another timeout family. Toy demonstration could validate the identity and sign reversal but must not simulate new empirical wins.
